## Design Context

### Users
ComiTe is currently a personal-stage project for people who already watch anime and want a better place to read manga, manhwa, and comics. The core job is to help readers discover titles, resume chapters quickly, track progress, and read comfortably across devices, especially on mobile. The interface should also respect power users who move fast and expect efficient navigation.

### Brand Personality
Calm, premium, playful.

The voice should feel confident and fandom-aware without becoming loud, cluttered, or novelty-driven. It should feel more like a polished collector product than a generic content portal.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode leads, but light mode must remain fully supported and intentional. Keep the current midnight/indigo/slate direction as the visual foundation. Avoid generic AI-generated aesthetics and avoid the usual noisy comic-reader tropes such as overcrowded chrome, cheap visual effects, and ad-site energy.

The product should feel mobile-first, reading-first, and quietly distinctive. Playfulness should come from selective details and tasteful moments of personality, not from constant visual noise.

### Design Principles
- Optimize for long reading sessions with low-clutter layouts, strong contrast, and comfortable scanning.
- Keep dark mode as the primary experience while maintaining full design parity in light mode.
- Favor premium restraint over portal clutter; remove anything that makes the product feel like a typical comic-reading site.
- Use playful fandom cues sparingly so the product keeps personality without losing polish.
- Design for mobile-first power users with touch-friendly controls, efficient navigation, and dense but readable information.
- Reuse the current token system and avoid AI-slop patterns, especially generic gradients, interchangeable card grids, and default-looking UI.
